## 1. Abstract

CARABAO is a culture-backed meme token inspired by the Filipino carabao — a symbol of strength, patience, resilience, and quiet labor. Unlike fast, hype-driven meme coins, CARABAO embraces a slow-burn philosophy: long-term conviction over short-term noise.

CARABAO exists to onboard Filipinos worldwide into crypto responsibly, promote financial education, support cultural preservation, and contribute to real-world causes — including the protection of the endangered tamaraw and sustainable farming innovation.

---

## 2. Vision

To become the **world’s most respected culture-backed meme coin**, representing Filipino resilience on a global stage — proving that patience, conviction, and community can outperform hype.

CARABAO envisions a future where:

* Filipinos confidently participate in the crypto economy
* Meme culture supports real education and real causes
* Decentralized communities act with responsibility and purpose

---

## 3. Mission

CARABAO’s mission is threefold:

1. **Crypto Education & Financial Responsibility**
   Introduce Filipinos worldwide to blockchain, DeFi, and digital assets through accessible, community-driven education.

2. **Cultural & Environmental Stewardship**
   Support awareness and protection of the endangered tamaraw and promote initiatives that enhance the genetic resilience of Filipino carabao.

3. **Technology Meets Tradition**
   Advocate for modern farming technologies while preserving Filipino agricultural heritage and cultural identity.
